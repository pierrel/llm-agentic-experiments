# Minimum adequate experiments avoid misleading support

- **Origin:** Pierre, experiment-lab setup conversation, 2026-08-23.
- **Intervention:** require an experimental-design reviewer to justify every
  model, harness feature, setting, task, replicate, metric, and external
  dependency as necessary to answer the registered question; remove or hold
  fixed everything else.
- **Prediction:** registrations will use smaller, more interpretable setups and
  will make cross-model or cross-architecture comparisons less confounded.
- **Boundary:** “minimum” must not mean underpowered or omit a control,
  measurement, safety containment, or fidelity requirement needed for the
  claimed inference.
- **Possible experiment:** independently design the same question with and
  without this review, then blind-score the resulting registrations for
  confounds, unnecessary moving parts, cost, and ability to reproduce the
  declared contrast.
