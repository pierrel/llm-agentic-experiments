# Durable commitment is a separate stage from capability routing

## Origin

Durable-promise capability routing v5, 2026-08-24.

## Observation

Across 24 sealed ordinary-web-main episodes, a general grounding boundary
changed routing only marginally (C1 6/12 versus C0 5/12) and produced no
durable commitment writes in either condition. Both conditions could sometimes
retrieve local facts, especially on lower-conflict rows, but neither reliably
converted the later conditional request into `/agent/memory.md` after the
checked result.

## Hypothesis

Mixed requests need general guidance that treats a future user-directed action
as a durable outcome to complete after its required evidence is checked, rather
than merely as text to answer alongside the current question. A treatment that
makes this stage explicit should increase post-grounding private commitments
without weakening grounding-first routing or causing unsupported “saved” claims.

## Boundary

The intervention must not name the pilot tasks, date skills, or a particular
competing tool. It should not turn every conditional statement into memory, nor
write before the needed fact is verified. This pilot's rows are observed and
cannot serve as confirmation evidence for the new hypothesis.
