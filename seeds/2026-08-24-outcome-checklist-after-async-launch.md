# A checklist after async launch may preserve independent outcomes

- **Origin:** The 2026-08-24 ordinary-web product probe of the durable-memory
  treatment was 0/3 on its strict mixed grounding-plus-commitment row. The
  frozen route requires `load_skill(grounding)` and the context-task launch
  before other dependent work, so a checklist cannot be forced before launch
  without changing the established grounding lifecycle.
- **Intervention:** after the agent launches local or asynchronous work for one
  of several independently valuable user outcomes, append general todo-layer
  guidance to make a short temporary checklist, reconcile it before replying,
  and keep durable state in its proper store rather than in the todo list.
- **Prediction:** on a fresh ordinary-web route, the model will retain the
  future conditional action through the context result and answer synthesis,
  while preserving the existing grounding order and answer quality.
- **Boundary:** a single direct outcome, trivial few-step work, and a user who
  explicitly requests a user-file todo list should keep the default behavior.
  The hypothesis does not justify a special memory mechanism, a task-specific
  reminder prompt, or treating todo completion as durable persistence.
