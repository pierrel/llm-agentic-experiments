# Proposed Assist roadmap item: no product guidance change; improve experiment oracle design

## Proposed outcome

No Assist guidance or architecture change is proposed. The development series
does not establish a context-length behavior loss.

## Evidence and limits

The three result capsules show that literal phrase checks changed apparent
outcomes independently of dose. That supports only lab-process work: a future
held-out context study needs a separately calibrated outcome oracle. Current
Assist guidance and behavior must remain stable.

## Product action

Propose a lab-roadmap item, not an Assist feature: “Calibrate deterministic
and semantic handoff-oracle agreement on held-out outputs before measuring a
guidance or context intervention.” Do not schedule product implementation from
this exploratory result.
