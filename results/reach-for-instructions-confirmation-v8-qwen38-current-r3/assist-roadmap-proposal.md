# Proposed Assist roadmap item

## Proposed outcome

Add a follow-up Assist research item: test progressive, matched procedural
guidance at several rendered-context doses around the V8 high-context region.
Keep the current model, reasoning profile, Deep Agents architecture, tool
schema, task shape, and procedure fixed; vary only sealed inert context and
guidance delivery.

## Evidence and limits

This capsule shows G02 30/36 versus G01 25/36 overall, with 11/12 versus 5/12
at high context. It is sufficient to justify a research roadmap item, not a
product prompt change. Adjacent low- and medium-context behavior must remain
measured because the advantage is not uniform.

## Product action

Do not change Assist guidance from this result. Register the dose-refinement and
fresh-task confirmations first; only propose an Assist experiment after Pierre
reviews the durable learning.
