# Held-out confirmation V1: invalid before completion

V1 began eight sealed episodes before an artifact review detected that the
oracle preflight had omitted two direct grounded forms the model was producing:
`approved_not_executed` for an approved removal that has not run, and a
parenthetical role after the verified owner name, such as `Marisol (security
owner)`. The retained artifacts otherwise contained the requested account,
next action, and unresolved asset-tag uncertainty. This repeats the earlier
lesson that a small preflight set can still be too narrow.

The cohort was stopped rather than consume the remaining 64 episodes under an
invalid measure. The local private raw run has eight admissions and eight
terminal `artifact_failure` records, all preserved without interpretation. Its
bundle digest is `99a7e56c1839030009aaa51f59dac3c2962b6c7277ad244d94d6c5f0f9fb9016`;
the admission and outcome-chain file hashes are respectively
`17a81cff10d17a8e0d78365693dc5c40967c1f563a18cb761c5f4b56843d9ce9` and
`f31a095bebd3caff7265d94041b2385280206b46a32e5e2eefe29ffb4e0ff62e`.

V1 is not a completed result capsule and its counts are not evidence about
guidance delivery. V2 is a new registered calibration rerun: it adds only
these observed grounded forms to a fresh preflight and runs a fresh full cohort.
