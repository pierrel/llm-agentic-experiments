# Durable-promise orchestration v1

Status: development experiment. Registered before new model episodes.

## Question

Can a concise addition to the existing todo guidance improve completion of an
ordinary mixed turn: a local factual answer plus a user-named later response,
without weakening the grounding lifecycle or making a todo list stand in for
durable thread state?

## Fixed setting

Each episode uses the current Assist ordinary web-main graph, the local Qwen
model, a fresh sandbox/workspace/thread, the existing deterministic async task
fixture, and no live search. The control is the exact current prompt and tool
configuration. A candidate differs only in its declared todo-guidance prose.

## Frozen task split

Development rows are the existing leading, trailing, and differently phrased
future-check-in compound prompts. Holdouts are the makerspace reminder,
internet-bill reminder, and forced-compaction follow-up. The rows and their
oracles are copied unchanged from the previous durable-promise branch before
any candidate is admitted.

## Unit and outcome

One fresh run of one frozen row under one condition is an experimental unit.
The primary outcome is the frozen full-row pass: grounding lifecycle, supplied
local answer, private durable commitment, and supported final reply. Todo use
and final todo states are secondary manipulation checks only.

## Schedule and stopping

For each candidate, run matched control and candidate N=3 on the development
rows in a predeclared alternating order. A candidate advances only when it is
at least 70 percent pooled and does not lose to its matched control on a
primary row. A finalist receives untouched-holdout N=5, full frozen-panel
N=10, and a representative general regression panel N=3. Stop when that bar
is met or after at most ten distinct candidate texts/configurations. Admission
or harness failures are recorded but are not candidates.

## Exclusions and interpretation

Production-admission denials, malformed harness episodes, and missing
manipulation exposure are administrative failures, not behavioral outcomes;
repair and repeat the same scheduled episode. Timeouts, refusals, and ordinary
agent failures count. This frozen family is evidence of stability for these
mixed local-and-commitment shapes, not a broad statistical proof about all
durable memory behavior.

## Conditions initially allowed

1. Minimal todo-layer rider: independent user-visible outcomes plus tool,
   subagent, or waiting work may warrant one short checklist; reconcile before
   reply; a todo is not durable storage.
2. A shorter equivalent rider.
3. One-user-outcome-per-todo wording.
4. Response-completeness reconciliation wording.

Further conditions require a new amendment that names the observed failure
pattern. Delegation is reserved for a failed, genuinely exposed todo series.
