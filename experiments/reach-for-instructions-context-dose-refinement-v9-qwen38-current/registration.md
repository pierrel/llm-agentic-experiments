## Outcome and interpretation

The primary outcome is the unchanged condition-blind deterministic
fixture-grounded JSON handoff plus ordered inventory/read/write procedure. The
secondary process measure is the exact named skill load before the first source
record read, even on a failed primary artifact. Report every reason-coded
outcome, cell pass count, first provider-request tokens, and process count.

This is a descriptive response-curve confirmation, not a powered threshold
estimate. It may establish only whether V8's high-dose separation recurs or
changes across these four fixed doses for this model, task, and harness. It
cannot establish a universal threshold, product Assist effect, or transfer to
another task, model, reasoning profile, or architecture.
