# Reach-for-instructions confirmation V8 design review

## Minimum adequate setup

V7 is retained as a partial, non-diagnostic execution because its
preregistered oracle made a supervisor name mandatory in an approval-status
field. The repair is a fresh fixture and a fresh calibration, not a post hoc
change to V7. V8 retains one private Deep Agents filesystem/tool loop, one
loader schema, three context doses, 12 trials per cell, and the current Assist
Qwen3.8 profile. It adds no agent, judge, tool, product prompt, or task-family
axis.

## Reviews and resolved findings

- **Scientific integrity:** A fresh Northridge Labs fixture and seed separate
  V8 from V7. The condition-blind corpus was written before V8 admission and
  accepts a direct and a compact source-grounded form. V7 is neither altered
  nor pooled.
- **Statistical rigor:** The experimental unit is one fresh episode. The
  fixed, interleaved 72-episode screen reports all terminal outcomes; 12 per
  cell supports descriptive comparison, not a powered interaction or a
  threshold claim. GPU denials are administrative attempts and retry the same
  scheduled unit. No result-dependent stopping rule is registered.
- **Agentic harness fit:** Both arms retain the same loader schema, filesystem
  tools, and user task. G01 has the procedure directly; G02 has only a matched
  catalog entry and must call the loader. Each worker verifies the captured
  post-middleware request against its sealed digest.
- **Oracle validity:** The requested field is approval status, not supervisor.
  The V8 compact calibration therefore requires conditional approval, photo,
  and pending state but not Talia. It rejects an approval after the photo is
  confirmed, bare approval, completion, and an unsupported label assertion.
- **Minimum setup:** A second task family, a larger catalog, extra treatments,
  a full Assist thread, or an LLM judge would not resolve the observed boundary
  and would add new causal factors. The retained model, architecture, context
  doses, ordering check, and process measure are necessary to preserve the
  original confirmation question and its known context condition.
- **Pre-admission integrity r2:** Before any V8 model request, the calibration
  adds contradictory-completion, embedded-assertion, and explicitly-pending
  photo cases. The bundle now records the registration and report hashes, and
  admission verifies the tagged contents of those files plus every shared
  harness module used by the runner.
- **Oracle hardening r3:** The condition-blind corpus now places an unsupported
  label claim in the action field as well as uncertainty, distinguishes both
  complete and completed from their negated forms, and rejects compact
  photo-confirmed, prefix-verb, and identifier-suffix variants.

## Decision

V8 is a narrowly corrected fresh confirmation. Its only new degree of freedom
is the independently preregistered compact approval representation needed to
measure the existing outcome. It cannot establish a broad Assist design rule
or a model-transfer result by itself.
